Our Story

A Café Born From Passion

Nestled on Agra Road, Jaipur, Misti Bakery & Cafe is a cozy haven crafted for food lovers who crave something beyond the ordinary. We blend global flavours with local warmth — from crispy momos to wood-fired pizzas, sizzling Chinese to indulgent waffles.

Our rooftop seating offers breathtaking views of the Pink City's skyline — the perfect backdrop for your morning coffee, a casual lunch, or a late-night dessert date.
